# Geographic basemap

Natural Earth 1:110m land, public domain. Bundled for the keyless coordinate map and 3D globe; no external map requests are required. This is geography, not operational routes or inferred shipment positions.

Source: https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_110m_land.geojson

Terms: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/
